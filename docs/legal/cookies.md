# Cookies and similar technologies

_Last updated: 22/11/2026_

We only use non-essential cookies with your consent. You can change your choice at any time from **Cookie settings** in the footer. Refusing is as easy as accepting, and the site works the same.

## Categories

| Category | Default | What | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Strictly necessary** | Always on | Session cookie (sign-in), security token, language, your cookie choice | Session to 6 months |
| **Security** | Always on | Cloudflare Turnstile and bot protection | Session |
| **Audience measurement (no cookie)** | Always on | Cloudflare Web Analytics: anonymous page counts, no cookie, no cross-site tracking | — |
| **Product analytics** | Off | PostHog (EU): pages visited, actions such as upload, like, share | 12 months |
| **Advertising** | Off | Ad network (none yet; named here once chosen): personalised ads and measurement. Without consent, ads are non-personalised | Up to 13 months |
