# Privacy policy

_Last updated: 22/11/2026_

Controller: Adrien Leygues (Bootleygues), individual publisher — privacy contact: aleygues@yahoo.fr.

## What we collect and why

| Data | Purpose | Legal basis | Kept for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Email, username, password hash, sign-in provider ID (Battle.net, Google) | Create and secure your account | Contract | Life of the account + 30 days |
| Captures, titles, captions, tags, comments, likes, follows, collections | Run the gallery | Contract | Until you delete them or your account |
| Links to your social networks (optional, public on your profile) | Show them on your profile | Contract | Until you remove them or delete your account |
| Secret quest progress and badges, Hall of Seekers entry (optional, you can leave it) | Run the secret quests | Contract | Until you delete your account |
| Download log (which capture you downloaded, per day) | Count each download once | Legitimate interest | 30 days |
| Moderation scores and decisions, reports, sanctions | Keep the community safe, meet legal duties | Legitimate interest; legal obligation (DSA) | 1 year after the decision, longer if a dispute is ongoing; pseudonymised if you delete your account |
| Keyed fingerprint (HMAC) of the email and sign-in IDs of a **banned** account | Prevent banned users from signing up again | Legitimate interest | 1 year |
| Staff action log (who did what in the back office) | Accountability of moderators and admins | Legitimate interest | 2 years |
| Terms acceptance (date, version) | Prove consent to the Terms | Contract | Life of the account |
| Waiting-list email (invite-only beta) | Invite you when the site opens | Consent | Until 3 months after public opening |
| Invitation (keyed fingerprint of the invited email) | Let invited people sign up during the beta | Legitimate interest | Until used or 14 days |
| Two-factor secrets and recovery codes (staff only, encrypted or hashed) | Secure staff accounts | Legitimate interest | While the staff role lasts |
| Bug reports (text, optional screenshot, page, browser, user ID) | Fix problems | Legitimate interest | 12 months |
| IP address (hashed), security logs, rate-limit counters | Prevent abuse and spam | Legitimate interest | 30 days (hash key rotated monthly); counters deleted when their window ends (at most 24 hours) |
| Product analytics (PostHog, EU) | Understand how the site is used | **Consent** | 12 months |
| Advertising identifiers | Personalised ads | **Consent** | See Cookies |
| Notification preferences, cookie choice | Remember your settings | Contract; legal obligation | Account life; 6 months for cookie choice |

Image metadata (EXIF, which may contain location) is removed at upload. We never sell your data.

## Who processes it (subprocessors)
Cloudflare (hosting, images, security — USA/global), Neon (database — AWS Europe Central, Frankfurt), Sightengine (automated image moderation — France/EU), Resend (emails — USA), PostHog (analytics — EU), Sentry (error tracking, no personal data sent — EU region), GitHub (bug report tracking, no email or name sent — USA), Google and Blizzard (only if you sign in with them), and the advertising network once one is chosen (this policy will name it).
When you choose a password, we check it against known breaches with Have I Been Pwned using only the first 5 characters of a hash of your password: your password never leaves our servers.
Transfers outside the EU rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

## Your rights
Access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, portability, and withdrawal of consent at any time. Delete your account from your settings (effective after 30 days, cancellable). To export your data, write to aleygues@yahoo.fr (self-service export coming later). We answer within one month. You can complain to the CNIL (cnil.fr).

## Minors
The service is not intended for children under 15.

## Security
Passwords are hashed, traffic is encrypted, access to data is limited to what each service needs.
