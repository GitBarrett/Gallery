# Writing

- **Languages**: English by default, full French. No hard-coded text in a component or a page: everything goes
  through i18n (`i18n/en.json`, `i18n/fr.json`). Only the name "Legendary Shots" is never translated.
- **Keys**: grouped by feature (`layout.signIn`), written as literals (`t('layout.signIn')`) so that the
  completeness test (`tests/i18n.test.ts`, US-62.3) sees them; a key missing in either file fails the build.
  `ui` components take their texts as props from the caller.
- **Tone**: a player talking to a player. Clear, warm, a touch of the universe ("Quest log", "Accept quest")
  without ever getting in the way of understanding. No technical jargon.
- **Buttons**: a verb and its object ("Upload a capture", "Sign in"), never "OK" or "Submit" alone.
- **Errors**: say what happened and how to get out of it ("Too short: 12 characters minimum"), without blame.
- **Empty states**: say what is missing and offer the action that fills it (EmptyState).
- **Capture quality**: always the tier name and its rule ("Rare · 100+ likes"), never the colour alone.
- **Brands**: no Blizzard logo, icon or name in the interface outside the legal notice of the footer.
