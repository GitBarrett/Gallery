# Accessibility

Rules from US-75 and `docs/07-design.md`, to follow in every component and every screen. The **Accessibility** panel
of each story (axe) reports issues during development; component tests and Playwright tests make them blocking.

## Colour and contrast

- Normal text at 4.5:1 at least, large text (24 px and more) at 3:1: token pairs are checked by
  `tests/design-tokens.test.ts`.
- Information is never carried by colour alone: a quality tier also shows its name, a form error a message.
- **Never put text directly on a photo**: always a scrim or a solid background (07-design.md § Texte sur image).

## Keyboard and focus

- Everything clickable is reachable by keyboard, in reading order; nothing is available on hover only.
- Focus is always visible (`--focus-ring`); never remove it without replacing it.
- A modal or a bottom sheet traps focus, closes with Escape and gives focus back to the element that opened it
  (US-75.2): use Modal and BottomSheet, never a hand-made overlay.
- An icon-only control is an ActionIcon, named by its `label`.

## Targets and layout

- Touch targets of at least 44 × 44 px (`--control-md`).
- No horizontal scrolling at 360 px.
- Reduced motion respected: `base.css` neutralises animations and transitions.

## Content

- Every image has alternative text; a decorative image has `alt=""`.
- Every field has a visible, tied label; hint and error are announced (`aria-describedby`, `aria-invalid`).
- Headings follow the page hierarchy (a single `h1`, no skipped level).
